PHOTOGRAPHS (all genuine photographs of the Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut, from Wikimedia Commons)
Used: graded, cropped, animated with camera moves. On studio shots the show-floor background was removed
(BiRefNet segmentation) and replaced with a dark studio backdrop and floor reflection made from the car
itself. Number plates were blurred. The car's body, wheels, lights and proportions are untouched: no
AI-generated vehicle imagery anywhere.
