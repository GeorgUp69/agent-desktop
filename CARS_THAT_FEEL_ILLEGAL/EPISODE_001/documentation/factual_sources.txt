Labels: [MFR] manufacturer claim · [VERIFIED] independently measured/verified result ·

All of these figures belong to the Jesko ABSOLUT (low-drag, top-speed variant). The Jesko ATTACK is the
track variant with the large rear wing; its figures are not used except where the video contrasts them.
