CARS THAT FEEL ILLEGAL — EPISODE 001
Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut
Final narration script (as recorded) with on-screen text
Runtime: 88.0 s · 9:16 · 1080x1920 · 30 fps

Pronunciation: Koenigsegg = "KER-nig-seg" (Swedish ö), Jesko = "YES-ko", Absolut = "AB-so-loot".

Changes from the brief's draft script, and why:
- "Under the hood sits..." became "Behind the driver sits...". The Jesko is mid-engined; the V8 sits behind
  the cabin, not under a hood.
- Scene 4 names the verified manufacturer drag figure (0.278 Cd) instead of a generic "reducing drag".
- Scene 5 keeps the brief's intent ("speeds most supercars can only dream about") but says plainly that
  Koenigsegg's 500+ km/h figure is a simulation that has never been officially verified, then gives the one
  speed result that has been independently verified (0-400-0 km/h in 25.21 s).
- Scene 6 says "fully homologated road car", which is Koenigsegg's own wording ("fully homologated car").
